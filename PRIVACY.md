# Privacy policy — RAM Watch

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

**Short version: RAM Watch collects nothing, stores nothing about you off your phone, and sends nothing anywhere.**

## What the app reads
To show its readings, the app reads on your device only: memory, CPU, GPU, battery, storage, display, and network status (connection type, signal strength, data speed). These values are shown on screen and are never saved to a server or shared.

## What the app stores
Your settings (which stats to show, overlay position, update interval) and a few simple counters used to decide whether to show a suggestion (for example, how often RAM was above 90%). They stay in the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall it.

## Internet use
RAM Watch does not use the internet itself and has no analytics, ads or tracking SDKs. If you tap a suggestion card or a link in the About section, it opens in your web browser; that website's own privacy policy then applies (for example, Amazon's).

## Permissions
- **Notifications** — to show the pinned monitor notification.
- **Display over other apps** (optional) — for the game overlay and status-bar text.
- **Foreground service** — to keep the monitor running while pinned.
- **Network and Wi-Fi state** — to show connection type, speed and signal.
- **Start at boot** — to restore the pinned monitor after a restart, if you had it on.

## Children
The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
